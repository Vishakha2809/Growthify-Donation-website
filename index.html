FAQ

Questions NGOs usually ask

Do the receipts include 80G details?

Yes. Your 80G exemption text and PAN are printed on every receipt, exactly as you give them to us during setup.

Can more than one person use it?

Yes. Each staff member gets their own login, and every receipt shows who created it. Logins can be added or switched off at any time.

Does the donor get a copy of the receipt?

Yes. When a donation is saved, the donor receives an SMS with a link to view and download their receipt as PDF. You can also print it in the office.

Can we record items instead of money?

Yes. In-kind donations have their own receipt series with item name, quantity and approximate value for each item.

Can we get reports for our CA?

Yes. Download donations for any date range as an Excel file, and see the total collection between two dates on the dashboard.

Do we need internet?

Yes, an internet connection is needed so that data is saved safely on the server and SMS receipts can be sent.

How much does it cost?
